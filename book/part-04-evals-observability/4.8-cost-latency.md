## Why this matters

Cost and latency are product requirements, not afterthoughts, and agents can burn surprising amounts of both. Tracking them per task tells you where to optimize.

**Learning goals**

- Compute cost per task from usage
- Find latency hotspots in traces
- Set per-task budgets
